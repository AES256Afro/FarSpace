# Playing FarSpace

A short guide by career. Every key is also listed on the title screen under
CONTROLS, and WHAT'S NEW lists recent changes.

## First hour

Flight School walks you through thrust, docking, the market, a mission, a
jump, your ship's deck and dropping a rover. Pick a **call sign** on the title
screen: it puts you on the Fleet Wire and the leaderboards, and lets other
pilots in your system see your ship. Autosave runs on every dock and jump;
CLOUD: CREATE SAVE CODE lets you continue on another machine.

## Trader

- Buy where TREND says LOW, sell where it says HIGH. The market remembers every
  station you dock at and shows the best known sell price and the best known
  run from where you are.
- **Rare goods** (marked +) are made at one station each and are worth more the
  further you carry them.
- Watch the MISSIONS tab for the daily contract and the weekly community goal
  (a sales premium at one station type).
- Illegal goods (marked *) fence at black markets in Veil and pirate-heavy hubs;
  elsewhere customs may seize a sale.
- Fit a Cargo Rack, park a second hull with K in SHIPS, and name your ship
  with N.

## Explorer

- Every arrival logs the system (nav log). Hold V in-system for a detailed
  scan, survey worlds from orbit, and be first to a system for a first
  discovery tag everyone sees.
- Sell exploration data at the SURVEY tab; research posts pay 25% more.
- Fit a Fuel Scoop and refuel from stars (watch the heat bar), a Discovery
  Scanner to log systems fully on arrival, and a Surface Scanner for faster,
  richer orbital surveys. The Albatross Explorer hull has the scanner built in.
- Drop a rover (L from orbit) on any region: scan alien flora (hold V), recover
  crashed probes, chart regions and biomes for the codex.
- J engages cruise for long transits; N flies your plotted course.

## Miner

- Point at a rock and hold the mining key. Prospector Limpets read a rock's
  yield; some rich rocks are **cores** that only a seismic charge (C) opens.
- A Refinery turns part of every rock into metals; Collector Limpets pull the
  loot in.
- Rocks drop **materials** (iron, nickel, carbon, germanium, vanadium,
  polonium). Spend them at the ENGINEER tab of research and refinery stations
  on permanent upgrades: jump range, drives, scoop, mining, cargo, shields,
  heat vents, rover suspension and battery.
- The Ox Mining Barge hull cracks rocks fastest and carries the most.

## Squadron

- Pick a 2-5 letter tag on the title screen. Members rank together on the WIRE
  tab, /s talks on the squadron channel anywhere, and kills near a wingmate
  are shared.
- Pooled standing makes the best squadron a faction's **patron**: members trade
  like allies and get half-price yards there.
- Fund the treasury from the BASE tab of any station and buy a civilian
  station as your **base**: shared vault, half-price (or free, with a Fuel
  Depot) services, a defense grid, a market stake. A weekly base contract and
  a weekly squadron bounty pay the treasury. Undefended bases draw raiders.

## Combat, when it finds you

Mouse aims the turret; left-click fires, right-click mines. R fires a homing
torpedo. Named corsair captains carry a 400 CR bounty. Damaged systems are
repaired on your own deck (I) with spare parts. Hardcore mode (Settings) makes
destruction erase the save.
